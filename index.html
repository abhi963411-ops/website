Privacy Commitment

FeelData Data Privacy Principles

At FeelData, we operate under a strict baseline of client trust and data sovereignty:

  • Data Ownership: Your business data belongs strictly to your business. FeelData acts solely as an analytical partner.
  • Permission-Based Governance: Access to data is bounded by clear, mutually agreed parameters.
  • No Hidden Monetization: We do not sell, transfer, or commercialize client data without explicit authorization.
  • Responsible Practices: We handle information with privacy-conscious methods focused solely on delivering your requested business insights.
